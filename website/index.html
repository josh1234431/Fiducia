Stereo DEM

Illustration of some behind the scenes magic

This is roughly what happens when you press Extract on the Stereo DEM page. P.S. you can drag the 3D model around

  1. 01

    Match

    Both photos are resampled so the same ground sits on the same row, then every pixel is searched for along that row in its partner by semi-global matching.

  2. 02

    Triangulate

    Each match becomes a pair of rays from the adjusted cameras, intersected in 3D.

  3. 03

    Merge

    Surfaces from every pair are levelled against each other and merged into one, ready for contours, hillshades and volumes.

Accuracy

Science first.

The primary goal of Fiducia is accuracy, because what use is a science software that is inaccurate? The software was built around algorithmic accuracy and has been thoroughly tested with both synthetic and real-world data. This ensures margins of error fall well within all accepted and expected limits. See key testing results below.

Features

  • Drone photo alignmentreading the camera, GPS positions and tie points from the photos
  • Drone blocks without ground controlsolving orientations and lens calibration together
  • Bundle adjustmentself-calibration, robust estimation and data snooping
  • GNSS and IMU observationsweighting camera positions by their stated precision
  • Accuracy to ASPRS 2023 and NSSDAstated from independent check points
  • Scanned filmdetecting fiducial marks automatically
  • Calibration certificatesextracting calibrations offline or with an AI model
  • Ground controlmeasuring by hand, importing survey tables or matching automatically
  • Least-squares tie pointsmatching to 0.04 px
  • Dense surface modelsmatching every well-overlapping pair of photos
  • Stereo DEMssemi-global matching on selected pairs
  • True orthophotosfilling hidden ground from photos with a clear view
  • Orthophotosgenerating orthophotos on any DEM in parallel
  • Mosaicsbalancing colour and routing seams where photos agree
  • Terrainfilling voids, shading relief, contouring and computing volumes
  • LiDAR ground classificationfinding the ground in unclassified point clouds
  • Canopy height modelsmeasuring heights above ground, pit-free under dense canopy
  • LiDAR noise filteringlabelling birds, multipath and stray returns as noise
  • Point classes from labelstraining a classifier on points labelled in a section view
  • Large point cloudsprocessing surveys of any size in bounded memory
  • LiDAR rastersrasterising and checking point clouds against a reference
  • Satellite scenesrefining rational polynomial models with control
  • Reportslisting residuals and accuracy, including the classic fixed-column layout
  • Autosave, snapshots and crash recoverysaving every edit as it is made
  • Multiple monitorsopening any photo or output in its own window
  • Open formatsreading and writing GeoTIFF, COG, PCIDSK, ERDAS Imagine and JPEG 2000
  • Any coordinate systemsupporting any EPSG system and the South African Lo zones
  • Local processingkeeping all data on the computer, with no uploads
Software design and libraries

Built from first principles.

The photogrammetric models (collinearity, resection, bundle adjustment and orthorectification) are my own implementation from first principles. Established open-source libraries handle the numerical optimiser, dense stereo matching, feature matching, and raster and projection work. Fiducia is open source too, under the Apache 2.0 licence, so anyone can read the maths, check it and build on it.
