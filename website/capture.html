Fiducia Capture

Photos for a 3D model, without the guesswork

Point, step and shoot. Capture keeps track of how far round you have got, and says straight away if a photo is blurred, too dark, too far from the last one, or taken from the same spot.

    The photos stay on this phone until you share them. Capture needs the camera, and asks for the motion sensors, which it uses to tell which way you are facing and whether you stepped between photos.
