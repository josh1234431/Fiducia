The name

Named for the marks that make a photograph measurable

fīdūcia · Latin · trust, confidence, reliance

Fiducial marks are the small registration marks exposed onto every frame of aerial film. Until they are measured, a scan is just a picture. Once they are, every pixel can be traced back to the camera and down to the ground.

What it promises

The software's job is to be trusted. Every edit is journalled before it is acknowledged, paths survive a drive letter changing, and a failed adjustment names the suspect point. The name makes that promise out loud.

How to write it

Fiducia, capital F, one word. Never FIDUCIA in running text, never fiducia except in code and file names. The project bundle keeps its .fidu extension.

The mark

A film frame, reduced to what you need to find its centre

On a metric camera, lines drawn between opposite fiducials cross at the principal point: the true centre of the photograph, and the origin every measurement is taken from. The mark keeps two of those fiducials and the point between them.

  1. 1
    The F is a fiducial

    The top-left corner mark, given a crossbar. It is the only letterform in the mark.

  2. 2
    The opposite fiducial

    The bottom-right corner closes the frame. The frame itself is implied, never drawn.

  3. 3
    The principal point

    Exactly where the diagonals cross. It is the one teal element in the logo.

App icon

Built on a 64-unit grid, legible down to 16 pixels

The dark tile is the default: it matches the workspace and keeps the photograph the brightest thing on screen. The light tile is for light documents and slides.

Colour

Grey surfaces, one teal

Imagery is the loudest thing Fiducia ever shows, so the interface stays in desaturated greys and uses a single teal for whatever needs registering: the principal point, the active step, the primary action.

Using the logo

Keep the point teal and the frame open

Do

Give the mark clear space on every side equal to the length of the F's crossbar. Keep the mark at 14 px or larger, and the icon at 16 px or larger.

Don't

Recolour the principal point. It is teal everywhere, including single-colour print, where it can drop to black but never to another hue.

Don't

Draw the frame or add the other two corners. The mark only works because the frame is implied.

Don't

Rotate, tilt or skew it. A fiducial frame is only useful when it is square to the image.

Voice

Plain, specific, and checkable

Fiducia talks like a careful surveyor: short sentences, real units, and claims the reader could verify. It names the problem and what to do about it, and it never apologises or sells.

Headline
A photograph is not a map.

States the problem the product exists to solve, in six words.

Promise
Pull the power out and you lose nothing.

A concrete claim instead of "reliable autosave".

Error
Point GCP-14 is 3.2 m from where the other 23 put it. Check it first.

Names the suspect, the size of the problem and the next step. Never just "Bundle adjustment failed."
